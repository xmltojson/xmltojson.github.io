Dear Community,

Ready to relive a timeless classic—with a woodland twist? 🐍🌿

We're excited to present **Snake — Woodland Trail**, a modern take on the beloved arcade game, featuring 20 progressive levels, procedurally illustrated scenery, and an intelligent AI explorer!

🎮 **WHAT'S INSIDE:**

🐍 **Classic Gameplay** — Gather apples, grow your snake, and avoid your own tail  
🌿 **Woodland Scenery** — Explore textured grass, scattered leaves, and rocky clearings  
🎯 **20 Progressive Levels** — Work your way from 3 apples to a 60-apple harvest  
🤖 **AI Explorer** — Watch automatic pathfinding with no time limit  
🏆 **Trail Records** — Track your best unassisted completed levels  
⏱️ **Time Challenges** — Reach each apple target before time runs out  
🪨 **Rocky Terrain** — Navigate around up to 15 woodland rocks  
💾 **Auto-Save** — Return to your saved trail and resume when ready  
🔊 **Optional Audio** — Enjoy gentle game tones as you play  
🐢 **More Breathing Room** — Levels 11–20 now run at 80% of their original movement speed  

🖥️ **TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:**

✓ Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no build dependencies  
✓ Canvas-based procedural graphics and smooth snake animation  
✓ Viewport-fitted desktop layout with compact panels  
✓ Responsive mobile design  
✓ Swipe gestures, on-screen buttons, and keyboard controls  
✓ BFS pathfinding, route simulation, and escape-route checks for AI  
✓ LocalStorage for saved progress, settings, and records  
✓ Automatic pause when leaving the browser tab  
✓ Reduced-motion support
