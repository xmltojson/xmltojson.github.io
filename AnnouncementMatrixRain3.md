We're thrilled to present our **Interactive Matrix Rain Web Application** — a cinematic digital rain simulation inspired by the iconic Matrix films. Bring the cyberpunk atmosphere to your browser with layered character streams, phosphor glow, and a scene you can make your own.

## 🎁 WHAT'S INSIDE:

- 🟢 **Cinematic Digital Rain** — Three depth layers with varying character sizes, speeds, and brightness
- 🌌 **Scene Presets** — Choose **Cinematic**, **Classic**, or **Deep Focus** for an instant change of atmosphere
- 🎨 **Color Themes** — Phosphor Green, Classic Green, Ice Blue, Ultraviolet, Amber, Crimson, and custom colors
- 🔤 **Character Sets** — Katakana + numerals, Binary, Hexadecimal, Kanji, Symbols, and your own custom characters
- ✨ **Phosphor Glow** — Bright leading glyphs with adjustable bloom and fading trails
- 🎞️ **Film Effects** — Optional grain, scanlines, and a cinematic vignette
- 🌈 **Spectrum Colors** — Fill your scene with multicolored streams
- ⚙️ **Live Customization** — Adjust speed, density, character size, trail length, brightness, and bloom
- 📷 **PNG Screenshots** — Capture the rain without interface controls
- 💾 **Remembered Preferences** — Visual settings, panel visibility, and playback state restore after reloading

## 🖥️ TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:

- ✓ Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — zero dependencies
- ✓ Canvas rendering with quality modes targeting **up to 60 FPS**
- ✓ **Low Power mode** targeting up to 30 FPS
- ✓ Cached glyph rendering and downsampled bloom
- ✓ High-DPI support with rendering-resolution limits
- ✓ Responsive desktop and mobile interface
- ✓ Glass-style settings panel, **collapsed on your first visit**
- ✓ Animation stops while the tab is hidden
- ✓ Reduced-motion support, labeled controls, and visible keyboard focus

*Actual performance depends on your device, browser, and selected settings.*

## ⌨️ QUICK CONTROLS:

| Key | Action |
