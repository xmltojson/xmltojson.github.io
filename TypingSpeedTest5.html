A quick guide to your typing practice.

Getting started

  1. Choose a language and a 30, 60, or 120-second test.
  2. Click Start test. The timer begins with your first typed character.
  3. Copy the passage exactly. Backspace and corrections are welcome.
  4. Each full-length passage advances automatically, even if it contains mistakes.
  5. The test ends when time runs out or all eight passages are finished.

Understanding your results

CPM is the number of correct characters remaining in your typed text per minute. WPM is CPM divided by five. Spaces and punctuation count.

Accuracy measures correct character-entry attempts. Each newly entered wrong character adds an error; correcting it does not erase that attempt. Deletions do not add errors. Pasting and dropping text are disabled.

Settings and navigation are locked during a test. Reset discards the current attempt. The timer continues if you switch browser tabs.

Stars and points

★★★: at least 95% accuracy and 200 CPM.
★★: at least 85% accuracy and 150 CPM.
★: finish a test with at least one correct character.

Points = CPM × accuracy + 50 per star + 20 per completed passage. Accuracy is used as a fraction. A test with no correct characters earns no points or stars.

Daily visits and practice days

Each new daily visit earns one star and your visit streak × 10 points. Calendar practice days count only days with a completed test. Results and unlocked achievements are stored locally in this browser.
