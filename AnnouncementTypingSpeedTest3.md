---

🎁 **WHAT’S INSIDE:**

⌨️ **Live Typing Feedback** — Track CPM, WPM, accuracy, and errors as you type  
🌍 **4 Languages** — English, Ukrainian, Russian, and German  
📖 **32 Practice Passages** — Eight per language, shuffled for each test  
⏱️ **Flexible Sessions** — Choose 30, 60, or 120 seconds  
▶️ **First-Keystroke Timer** — Get comfortable; timing begins when you type  
📅 **Practice Calendar** — Explore daily activity and monthly summaries  
📈 **Personal Statistics** — Follow your speed trend and celebrate personal bests  
💾 **History & CSV Export** — Save up to 100 recent results and download your data  
🔥 **Daily Visit Rewards** — Build a visit streak and collect bonus stars and points  
🏆 **12 Achievements** — Unlock milestones for speed, accuracy, and consistency  
🌿 **Refreshed Design** — A workspace-inspired interface with a visual keyboard guide

🚀 **GET STARTED:**

1. Choose your language and session length.
2. Click **Start test**.
3. Type your first character to begin the timer.
4. Follow the passage, correct mistakes, and find your rhythm.
5. Review your results and try again.

**Accuracy first. Speed will follow.**
