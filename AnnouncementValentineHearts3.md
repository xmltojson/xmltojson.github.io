Love is in the air! 💕

We're thrilled to present our **Interactive Valentine Hearts Web Application** — a little love, a little magic, and a heartfelt way to brighten someone’s day.

💝 **WHAT'S INSIDE:**

💕 **Floating Hearts** — Catch gently drifting hearts to discover love notes  
💖 **Heart Burst** — Fill the heart garden with floating hearts and sparkling particles  
💌 **Personal Touches** — Add your name and your favorite person’s name  
💬 **Love Notes** — Enjoy 10 romantic notes or add up to 20 of your own  
✨ **Heart Magic** — Tap the big heart for a new note and a burst of love  
🎵 **Soft Chimes** — Optional gentle sounds for heart interactions  
🔗 **Shareable Valentines** — Create a link with your names, notes, and settings  
⚙️ **Make It Yours** — Customize heart size, drift duration, and frequency  
⏸️ **Your Pace** — Pause the animation or turn off automatic notes  

🖥️ **TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:**

✓ Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no dependencies  
✓ Single-file application with an embedded SVG favicon  
✓ URL-based configuration sharing  
✓ LocalStorage persistence  
✓ Viewport-fitting main layout without page-level vertical scrolling  
✓ Mobile and desktop layouts, including portrait and landscape  
✓ Keyboard controls and reduced-motion support  
✓ Social sharing and native sharing where available  
✓ Toast notifications  

🎯 **PERFECT FOR:**

• Valentine's Day greetings  
• Romantic surprises for loved ones  
• Anniversary messages  
• A little self-love and encouragement  
• Sharing personalized notes with someone special  
• Educational coding demonstrations  

🔗 **SHARE YOUR LOVE:**

Select **Make It Yours**, add your personal touches, and share your Valentine:

• 📋 Copy the link to your clipboard  
• 💬 Send via WhatsApp  
• ✈️ Share via Telegram  
• 📧 Send by Email  
• 📱 Use your device’s share sheet when available  

Your link carries your names, notes, and atmosphere settings — ready for someone special to discover. 💕

Remember: anyone with the link can read the included notes, so keep private details out of your Valentine.
