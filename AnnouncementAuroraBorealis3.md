Dear Community,

The Northern Lights have a new home on your screen! ✨

Meet **Northlight — Aurora Borealis**, an interactive web application that brings the atmosphere of an Arctic night to your browser.

Watch delicate curtains of light drift above snow-covered mountains, shimmer across a quiet lake, and disappear into a sky full of stars. A little space to pause, explore, and enjoy the view.

🎁 **WHAT’S INSIDE:**

🌌 **Flowing Aurora Curtains** — Fine, layered light rays with natural-looking folds and a soft glow  
⭐ **Twinkling Starfield** — Stars with varied brightness, size, and subtle color  
🌠 **Occasional Meteors** — Brief shooting stars with fading trails  
🎨 **8 Color Palettes** — From naturalistic oxygen green to artistic violet, crimson, and ice blue  
🏔️ **Procedural Arctic Landscape** — Snowy ridges, shadowed rock faces, pine silhouettes, and shorelines  
🌊 **Lake Reflections** — The sky and mountains mirrored in gently rippling water  
⚙️ **Personalized Settings** — Adjust movement, brightness, curtain layers, star density, and exposure  
📷 **Save Your Favorite Moment** — Pause the scene and export an image without interface controls  
🎲 **Discover a Scene** — Generate a fresh landscape and aurora combination  
💾 **Remembered Preferences** — Settings saved locally when browser storage is available  

🖥️ **TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:**

✓ Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no external libraries  
✓ Procedural HTML5 Canvas rendering — no external images  
✓ Responsive interface for desktop and mobile  
✓ Adjustable rendering quality  
✓ Keyboard controls and reduced-motion support  
✓ Animation pauses rendering in hidden tabs  
✓ Single-file application with no build process  
✓ Open source under the MIT License  

🎯 **PERFECT FOR:**

• A quiet break during a busy day  
• An atmospheric background for unwinding  
• Creative coding and generative art inspiration  
• Exploring procedural landscape techniques  
• Capturing a unique digital night sky  
• Enjoying a little Arctic wonder from home  

🔗 **Experience Northlight:** https://auroraborealisj.github.io

**No downloads. No signups. Just open your browser and look north.**

This is an artistic visualization of an imaginary Arctic night—not a live aurora feed or forecast.

Wishing you moments of wonder and tranquility beneath the digital Northern Lights!
