Let's light up the sky! 🎆

We're thrilled to present **Interactive Fireworks Show · Nightfall** — a canvas-based fireworks simulation that brings the magic of a pyrotechnic display right to your screen. Click or tap the sky and watch it glow above a moonlit city!

🎁 **WHAT'S INSIDE:**

🎆 **9 Explosion Shapes + Random Mode** — Sphere, Heart, Star, Ring, Double Ring, Willow, Chrysanthemum, Palm & Spiral  
🎨 **10 Color Palettes** — Rainbow, Red, Blue, Green, Gold, Purple, Pastel, Fire, Ice & White  
✨ **Glowing Trails & Glitter** — Luminous spark trails with optional shimmering effects  
💥 **Volley & Finale** — Launch five rockets in quick succession or trigger a spectacular grand finale  
🔄 **Auto Mode** — Sit back and enjoy a continuous show  
🔊 **Procedural Sound FX** — Rising launch sounds and booming explosions, with adjustable volume  
💫 **Ambient Light & Smoke** — Soft explosion glow and subtle drifting clouds  
🏙️ **Moonlit City Skyline** — Layered buildings, warm windows, and a star-filled sky  
🌊 **Water Reflections** — Fireworks reflected in gently rippling water  
⚙️ **Customizable Physics** — Adjust spark count, burst size, gravity, trails, and launch interval  
🌍 **Three Languages** — German, Ukrainian, and English  
🧘 **Motion Controls** — Pause anytime or enable reduced-motion mode for gentler effects  

🖥️ **TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:**

✓ Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no dependencies or build tools  
✓ Canvas rendering with device-aware pixel scaling  
✓ Delta-time physics with clamped time steps  
✓ Adaptive particle density based on device performance  
✓ Cached glow sprites, particle pooling, and pre-rendered scenery  
✓ Rendering pauses when the tab is hidden or settings are open  
✓ Settings and language saved locally  
✓ Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet, and desktop  
✓ Glass-style controls and a keyboard-accessible settings dialog  
✓ Fullscreen mode where supported
