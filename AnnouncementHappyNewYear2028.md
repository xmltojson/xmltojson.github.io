We're thrilled to present our **Interactive New Year 2028 Web Application** — a digital celebration featuring realistic fireworks, golden confetti, and a beautiful moonlit waterfront.

🎁 **WHAT'S INSIDE:**

🎆 **Fireworks Display** — Multi-color bursts with glowing trails, subtle smoke, and water reflections  
🎊 **Confetti Shower** — Falling, rotating confetti for an extra festive touch  
🌙 **Moonlit Scenery** — A starry sky, textured moon, distant hills, and an illuminated waterfront skyline  
⏳ **New Year Countdown** — Count down to midnight on January 1, 2028, in your local time zone  
🕐 **Live Clock** — Localized date and time at a glance  
🎵 **Celebration Sounds** — Enjoy the original four-note festive chime, with a sound toggle  
💛 **Interactive Wishes** — Choose Health, Prosperity, Love, Happiness, Success, Peace, Strength, or Luck to reveal a heartfelt message  
🌍 **Three Languages** — German by default, followed by Ukrainian and English  
⏸️ **Your Celebration, Your Choice** — Pause animations or mute sound whenever you like  

🖥️ **TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:**

✓ Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — zero dependencies  
✓ Canvas-based scenery, fireworks, and confetti with device-pixel-ratio scaling  
✓ Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet, desktop, and landscape viewing  
✓ Time-based animation for consistent speeds across different refresh rates  
✓ Adaptive particle limits for less powerful devices  
✓ Saved language and sound preferences when local storage is available  
✓ Safe-area support for notched displays  
✓ Keyboard-friendly controls and screen-reader status messages  
✓ Reduced-motion accessibility support  
✓ Animation pauses automatically when the page is hidden
