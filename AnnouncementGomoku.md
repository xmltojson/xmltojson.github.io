We're excited to present our Interactive Gomoku Web Application - 
a beautifully crafted digital version of the timeless "Five in a Row" game, complete with an unbeatable AI opponent and a mesmerizing AI vs AI showcase.

🎁 WHAT'S INSIDE:

⚫⚪ Classic Gomoku Board - Elegant 15×15 wooden board with polished stone graphics
🤖 Unbeatable AI - Smart pattern-based opponent that recognizes threats & defends flawlessly
🎬 AI vs AI Mode - Sit back and watch two AIs battle, auto-restarting every 5 seconds
⏸️ Stop / Resume Control - Pause the AI showdown anytime and continue at will
👥 Local 2-Player Mode - Challenge a friend on the same device
🎯 Multiple Difficulty Levels - Easy, Unbeatable, and AI vs AI
🇺🇦 Ukrainian by Default - Full Ukrainian language support with English toggle
💾 Auto-Save - Game state, scores & settings persist across sessions
↩️ Undo & Live Scoreboard - Take back moves and track the running score
🎨 Polished Visuals - Gradient stones, last-move markers & animated win lines

🖥️ TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:

✓ Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript - Zero dependencies
✓ Canvas-based board rendering with DPR scaling
✓ Minimax AI with alpha-beta pruning (depth 4) & sophisticated pattern evaluation
✓ Controlled randomness for varied, unpredictable AI vs AI games
✓ Fully responsive design (mobile, tablet, desktop, portrait & landscape)
✓ Dynamic viewport height (dvh) so the board always fits — no clipping
✓ Local Storage persistence for game state & preferences
✓ Touch-friendly interactions optimized for small screens
✓ Safe-area insets for notched displays
✓ Scroll-free desktop layout with board & control panel side-by-side
