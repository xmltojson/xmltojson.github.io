We're delighted to present our **Interactive Christmas Tree Web Application** — a cozy digital winter scene where you can decorate your own tree, enjoy gentle snowfall, and listen to festive melodies.

🎁 **WHAT'S INSIDE:**

🎄 **Canvas-Rendered Tree** — Detailed pine branches, textured bark, and snow-covered boughs
🌙 **Moonlit Winter Scene** — A starry sky, snowy forest, and beautifully wrapped presents
❄️ **Gentle Snowfall** — Drifting snow you can switch on or off
💡 **Twinkling Lights** — 125 spiraling fairy lights with warm glows and subtle shimmer
🎨 **Glossy Ornaments** — 38 reflective baubles in five color palettes
🎁 **Decorate Your Tree** — Add up to 60 extra ornaments or restore the original decorations
⭐ **Golden Star Topper** — A faceted star that glows when the lights are enabled
🎵 **Christmas Melodies** — Five carol melodies synthesized with soft, bell-like tones
🎼 **Song Selector** — Jingle Bells, O Christmas Tree, Silent Night, Deck the Halls, and We Wish You a Merry Christmas
📊 **Music Visualizer** — Animated bars responding to the music
⚙️ **Settings Popup** — All your scene, language, and music controls in one place

🌍 **MAKE IT YOURS:**

✓ German by default, with Ukrainian and English available
✓ Your decorations and preferences saved locally in your browser
✓ Selected song and playback position restored after reloading
✓ Sequential playlist playback or repeat your favorite melody
✓ Adjustable volume and optional animation

> 🎵 If your browser blocks automatic music after a reload, click **Resume music** to continue from the saved position.

🖥️ **TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:**

✓ Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks or dependencies
✓ Canvas-based scenery, lights, ornaments, and snowfall
✓ Web Audio API synthesis — no audio files required
✓ Bell-like tones, dynamics compression, and frequency visualization
✓ LocalStorage for settings, decorations, and playback persistence
✓ Responsive layout for mobile and desktop
✓ Keyboard-accessible controls and reduced-motion support
✓ Music and animation pause when the page is hidden
