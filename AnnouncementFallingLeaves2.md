🍁 **WHAT'S INSIDE:**

🍂 **Detailed Falling Leaves** — Multiple leaf shapes with natural colors, delicate veins, and fluttering movement  
🌬️ **Natural Breeze** — Smooth wind effects with optional gusts  
☀️ **Golden Sunlight** — Warm light rays and soft atmospheric haze  
☁️ **Soft Clouds** — A gentle backdrop to the autumn woodland  
🌳 **Autumn Trees** — Detailed branches and colorful foliage framing a winding path  
🍃 **Interactive Leaf Scattering** — Click or tap the woodland to stir the leaves  
🍂 **Scatter Some Leaves** — Add a larger shower of leaves with one button  
💨 **Wind Indicator** — Follow the breeze from still to gusty  
⏸️ **Pause & Resume** — Enjoy the scene at your own pace  

🖥️ **TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:**

✓ Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no external dependencies  
✓ Procedurally drawn landscape and canvas-based leaf animation  
✓ Responsive design for mobile and desktop  
✓ Portrait and landscape support  
✓ Mouse, touch, and keyboard-accessible controls  
✓ Customizable leaf count, size, fall speed, tumble, and breeze  
✓ Settings automatically saved in localStorage  
✓ Settings panel minimized by default, with your expanded or collapsed preference remembered  
✓ Reduced-motion support  
✓ Animation pauses automatically when the browser tab is hidden
