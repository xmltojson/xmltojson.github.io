🐠 **Meet Aquarium Tank** 🌿

Dear Community,

**A small world. A little peace.**

I'm excited to share **Aquarium Tank**, an interactive aquarium web application that brings a little underwater calm to your screen. Watch fish explore, feed them, and create your own planted aquascape—all in your browser. 🌊

---

## 🐟 WHAT'S INSIDE

🐠 **Five Fish Species** — Neon tetras, angelfish, golden gouramis, silver barbs, and clownfish with distinctive markings and animated fins.

🍞 **Interactive Feeding** — Drop food and watch nearby fish swim toward it. Click a fish to discover its name, species, and bites eaten.

🌿 **Living Aquascapes** — Add ribbon grass, Amazon swords, stem plants, red ludwigia, sea fans, and anemones.

🪨 **Natural Decorations** — Shape your scene with river stones, weathered driftwood, moss, and an optional stone shelter.

💨 **Bubble Streams** — Add air stones and watch bubbles gently rise toward the surface.

☀️ **Daylight & Moonlight** — Switch the mood, adjust lighting and water current, and choose from three water palettes and four substrates.

✨ **Underwater Details** — Enjoy swaying plants, sunbeams, shimmering light patterns, floating particles, and surface ripples.

💾 **Your Aquarium, Remembered** — Fish, plants, and settings save automatically in your browser when local storage is available.

## 🖥️ TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS

✓ Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript—no external libraries  
✓ Procedurally drawn with HTML5 Canvas—no image assets  
✓ Responsive layout for desktop and mobile  
✓ Desktop layout designed for 100% browser zoom  
✓ High-detail and battery-friendly rendering modes  
✓ Fullscreen viewing where supported  
✓ Pause, resume, and keyboard controls  
✓ Respects reduced-motion preferences  
✓ Animation pauses when the tab is hidden
