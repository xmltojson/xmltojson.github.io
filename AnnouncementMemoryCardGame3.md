We're delighted to share **Memory Card Game** — a beautifully crafted photographic memory game designed for a calmer kind of play. Explore stunning nature photography, find matching pairs, and improve your personal best, all at your own gentle pace.

*Wander. Remember. Repeat.*

🎁 **WHAT'S INSIDE:**

🃏 Photo Matching — Flip and pair real nature photographs
🎨 Three Card Colors — Forest, Ocean & Plum palettes to suit your mood
📊 Three Difficulty Levels — Easy (8 pairs), Medium (12), Hard (18)
⏱️ Live Timer & Scoring — Track your moves, time & points with efficiency and speed bonuses
💡 Hint System — Up to 3 gentle hints when you need a little peek
⏸️ Pause & Resume — Take a breather; your timer waits for you
🏆 Personal Bests — Top scores saved per difficulty, right on your device
📷 Real Photography — A curated nature collection via Unsplash

🖥️ **TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:**

✓ Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript — Zero dependencies
✓ CSS 3D card flip animations with perspective transforms
✓ Elegant custom dialogs — no jarring native popups
✓ LocalStorage persistence — game state, high scores & theme saved
✓ Fully responsive — desktop, tablet & mobile optimized
✓ Graceful degradation — named cards stay playable if images can't load
✓ Smooth cubic-bezier transitions & keyframe animations
✓ Thoughtfully accessible — keyboard navigation, ARIA live regions & reduced-motion support
