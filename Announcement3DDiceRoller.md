Ready to let the dice decide? 🎲

We're thrilled to present our **3D Dice Roller Web Application**—a beautifully animated dice-rolling experience for tabletop games, friendly challenges, and little moments of chance. Pick your dice, give them a roll, and watch them tumble!

🎁 **WHAT'S INSIDE:**

🎲 **Animated 3D Dice** — Six-sided dice with realistic shading, inset pips, and soft shadows  
🔄 **Rolling & Bouncing Effects** — Watch your dice spin, bounce, and settle into place  
🎛️ **Custom Dice Count** — Roll anywhere from 1 to 8 dice at once  
➕ **Instant Totals** — See individual dice values and their combined sum  
🕘 **Recent Roll History** — Review your latest 8 rolls and clear them whenever you like  
🔊 **Optional Sound Effects** — Add synthesized dice impacts to every roll  
🇺🇦 **Ukrainian by Default** — A Ukrainian-first experience with English support  
🌍 **Simple Language Switcher** — Switch between **УК / EN** with one click  
💾 **Saved Preferences** — Keep your language, dice count, sound settings, results, and history between visits  
🎨 **Polished Dark Interface** — A textured dice tray, gradient backgrounds, and lavender accents  

📂 **GETTING STARTED IS SIMPLE:**

✓ Open the application—no installation or account required  
✓ Choose your dice count using the slider or **− / +** buttons  
✓ Click **Кинути кубики / Roll the dice**  
✓ Check your results and total  
✓ Enable sound for a more tactile experience  
✓ Your settings and roll history stay in your browser—no backend uploads  

🖥️ **TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS:**

✓ Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript—no external dependencies  
✓ CSS 3D dice with Web Animations API-powered rolling  
✓ Web Audio API-generated sounds—no audio downloads  
✓ Web Crypto-powered random results when available, with rejection sampling to avoid modulo bias  
✓ LocalStorage persistence with a graceful fallback when storage is unavailable  
✓ Compact desktop layout that minimizes page scrolling  
✓ Responsive layouts for mobile portrait and landscape  
✓ Scrollable history panel and touch-friendly controls  
✓ Keyboard-accessible controls and screen-reader result announcements  
✓ Support for reduced-motion preferences
